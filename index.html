A short guide

Understanding your reading

A few honest answers to the questions people usually have before or after their first reading.

What is Saju, actually?

Saju (사주, "four pillars") is a Korean system, rooted in centuries-old Chinese calendrical astrology, for reading someone's temperament and life pattern from the exact moment they were born — the year, month, day, and hour, each translated into a pair of Chinese characters (a "stem" and a "branch") drawn from a 60-step cycle. Those eight characters together are your 사주팔자 — "four pillars, eight characters."

Unlike Western astrology, which anchors everything to the Sun's position in the zodiac, Saju is built on Chinese Five Element theory (오행: Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water) and how those elements generate, feed, or restrain one another across your eight characters. A reading is really an exercise in balance-reading: which elements are strong, which are missing, and what that pattern tends to produce.

Do I really need my exact birth time?

It helps, but it isn't required. Your birth hour determines only one of the four pillars — the "hour pillar," which traditionally colors your later years and your children. The year, month, and day pillars (which carry most of the reading: your Day Master, your element balance, your Ten Gods, your 대운 life-decades) all come from your birth date alone.

If you don't know your birth time, leave it blank here — you'll still get a full three-pillar reading, just without the hour-pillar layer. It's genuinely worth asking a parent or checking a birth record if you can, though: the hour pillar is also what most affects your Day Master strength calculation, so an exact hour sharpens everything else too.

Saju vs. Western astrology — what's actually different?

Western astrology reads a single moment against the zodiac's twelve signs and the positions of the planets. Saju reads four moments at once — year, month, day, hour — each rendered as a pairing from a 60-step cycle, working out to 518,400 possible eight-character charts in total (more on that math in the articles below). It's less "which sign are you" and more "what is the elemental weather of the exact hour you arrived."

The other real difference is emphasis: Saju spends most of its attention on your Day Master (the stem of your day pillar) — a single character meant to represent your innate self — and on how the other seven characters support, drain, challenge, or feed that one. Everything from your favorable element to your luck cycles (대운) is read relative to that anchor.

Your Day Master, explained

Your Day Master (일간) is the heavenly stem of your day pillar — one of ten possible characters, each carrying an element (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, or Water) and a polarity (Yang or Yin). It's treated as the closest thing Saju has to "you": not your whole chart, but the character everything else in the chart is read against.

From your Day Master, a reading derives your Ten Gods (십성) — how each of your other seven characters relates to you (as a peer, an output, a wealth source, an authority, or a support) — and your Day Master strength (신강/신약), a rough read of whether your chart is self-sufficient or leans on its surroundings. That strength reading is what determines your 용신, or favorable element: the one thing this specific chart benefits most from leaning into.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Fortune Lantern is built so that it simply doesn't need to collect your personal data to work. This page explains exactly what happens to the information you type in.

What you enter

To generate a reading, a compatibility result, or a wish, you may enter a name, birth date, birth time, gender, birthplace, and a short wish. All of these fields are optional except birth date, and none of them are required to just browse the site.

Where it goes: nowhere

Every calculation on this site — your Four Pillars, your Ten Gods, your compatibility reading, your lucky talisman — runs entirely in your own browser, using JavaScript that ships with the page. Nothing you type is sent to a server, because Fortune Lantern doesn't have one to send it to. Close the tab or refresh the page, and everything you entered is gone. We never see it, store it, or share it, because it's technically never in our possession in the first place.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses Google Analytics to understand overall traffic, such as how many people visit and which pages are popular. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects general information like your approximate location, device type, and browser — it does not receive anything you type into the reading, compatibility, or wish forms, since those never leave your browser in the first place. You can opt out using a browser extension like Google's "Analytics Opt-out," or by using your browser's tracking-protection or private-browsing settings.

Offerings (PayPal)

If you choose to leave a voluntary offering, the "Send via PayPal" buttons open PayPal.com in a new tab. That transaction happens entirely on PayPal's site, under PayPal's own privacy policy — Fortune Lantern never receives, sees, or stores your card, bank, or PayPal account details.

Your downloads

Your reading (via your browser's print/save-as-PDF function) and your lucky talisman (a PNG image) are generated and saved directly to your own device. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else.
