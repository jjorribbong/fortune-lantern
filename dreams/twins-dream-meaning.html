Twins Dream Meaning — Korean Interpretation (쌍둥이꿈)

Twins already appear on this site in the taemong (conception dream) tradition, where seeing double imagery can be read quite literally as foretelling an actual pair of children. Outside that specific pregnancy context, a twins dream is read more abstractly — through duality, balance, and choices with two genuinely comparable options.

Meeting a pair of twins is read as a decision or comparison currently sitting on your mind — two paths, two offers, or two people that feel closely matched, making the choice between them genuinely difficult rather than obvious.

Being one of a pair of twins yourself within the dream, or discovering you have a twin, is read introspectively — examining two sides of your own personality, or two competing instincts about how to handle a current situation.

Twins who look identical but behave very differently from each other is read as a reminder that two similar-looking options can lead to very different outcomes — worth paying closer attention to the details of a decision rather than assuming two apparently similar choices are actually interchangeable.
